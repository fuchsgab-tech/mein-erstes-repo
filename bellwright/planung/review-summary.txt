LENS planner
SUMMARY The basic rules always hold. I ran about 11,900 synthetic teams (N=0–20, all phases, growth 0/0.35/1, disableWeak on/off, research off, hunting off, only delivery, all jobs off, null max values, ties, superstar). In every run each worker had exactly one priority 1, no value fell outside 1–9 or "aus", nothing crashed, and from N≥3 every job with importance ≥2 had someone on 1–2. The algorithm itself has real weaknesses, though, and they hit the most common case: teams under 8 workers. Main jobs are assigned suboptimally (greedy plus pair swaps). Importance and the phase presets barely affect which jobs get a main worker. The coverage pass stacks priority 2s on one person. Several reason texts and one note say false or useless things. All runnable scripts, including a patched prototype that fixes points 1–6 and leaves the example team's plan unchanged, are in /tmp/claude-0/-home-user-mein-erstes-repo/463ad377-28a1-5da9-b964-5a5b652dc408/scratchpad/review/algorithm/ (planner.js = code extracted 1:1, harness.js, opt.js, compare.js, mkfixed.js -> planner_fixed.js / planner_mult.js).
